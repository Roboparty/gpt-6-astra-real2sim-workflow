# Detailed-room portability exposes appearance loss — 2026-09-29 15:48 UTC

The retained room's portable formats show substantial appearance changes under
the unchanged original camera/light/World rig. Blender-to-source-photo full RGB
MAE is **0.082986**, compared with **0.140484 for GLB** and **0.137857 for USD**.
The floor appears black in both portable renders. This is a diagnosed delivery
defect, not an improved reconstruction or a successful appearance acceptance.

## Fixed input and artifact-only export

The [registered protocol](PORTABILITY_PROTOCOL_20260929.json) fixes the existing
`shell_vent_002/shell_vent` model, canonical scene, photograph, two appearance ROIs,
851×638 raster, eight CPU samples and seed zero. No authoring parameter was fitted
or source model edited. All three groups—native, GLB and USD—remain in the report.

The exporter adds an explicit `--interchange-only` mode: it follows the same
Blender/GLB/USD path but stops before presentation and ID renders. Its audit says
those renders were not produced; this is not a complete visual-delivery verdict.
Existing default and geometry-only paths remain distinct, and the two restricted
flags cannot be combined. The detailed room exported all three files in 4.4991 s.
No speedup ratio is inferred from different fixtures or render settings.

## The 8.09 mm GLB finding is not a demonstrated surface deformation

The existing all-vertex strict gate passed `.blend` and USD but failed GLB on the
bed's X bound: **0.00809443 m** difference, attributable to `bed_duvet`. This
**2/3 format verdict is retained unchanged**.

A separate read-only topology inspection found four evaluated vertices not
referenced by any triangle, plus 13 vertices used only by degenerate triangles.
The outer X and lower Z extrema causing the discrepancy are loose vertices.
With a fixed 1e-12 m² nonzero triangle-area cutoff, source surface and GLB surface
AABB difference is **2.3842e-7 m**; source all-vertex and source surface bounds
differ by 8.09449 mm. The raw mesh already contains two loose vertices.

Thus the all-vertex result does not establish that the rendered duvet surface
shrank by 8 mm. It exposes a mismatch in what the bound measurement includes.
Surface AABB agreement still does not prove complete mesh/topology equivalence.
No source cleanup, threshold relaxation or replacement of the registered gate
occurred in this continuation. A future cleanup or surface-based metric needs
its own controlled comparison and must retain the current failure.

## Controlled appearance, with all failed attempts preserved

Each group reopens the original model. For imports, original geometry is removed
while the original camera/lights are retained; imported cameras/lights are removed.
The original World and color management are restored. This deliberately isolates
appearance under one rig; it does not validate portable light/camera behavior.

The initial renderer failed because Blender string-property RNA objects do not
have `is_array`. The [first amendment](PORTABILITY_DIAGNOSTIC_AMENDMENT_20260929.json)
fixes introspection and permits a **labeled diagnostic image** when the geometry
gate fails; it does not permit acceptance. That second attempt exposed two rig
issues: unnecessary matrix reassignment introduced small float roundoff, and
USD import edited the active World's node graph.

The [second amendment](PORTABILITY_RIG_AMENDMENT_20260929.json) requires an
unparented static rig, avoids rewriting its transforms, and gives importers a
temporary World to modify. The original rig's exact snapshot-equality requirement
remains unchanged. All three final groups retain identical before/after rig
snapshots; GLB is explicitly `rendered_geometry_failed`. All earlier failures
and their source/code hashes remain preserved in separate directories.

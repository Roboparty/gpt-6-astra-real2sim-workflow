## Changes available for reuse

- Canonical wall/floor/ceiling collision surfaces, rectangular openings, proper
  world/local transforms and rejection of unvalidated arbitrary wall proxies.
- Fixed-camera masks and overlays at build stages; failed frozen thresholds block
  geometry/appearance acceptance. Source images must belong to accepted inputs;
  held-out views cannot enter reconstruction feedback. External annotation bytes
  participate in cache invalidation.
- [Source-bound priors](../PHYSICAL_PRIORS.md) for density, optical initialization,
  contact coefficients and explicitly inferred hidden surfaces. Pair friction is
  applied to collision geoms and audited after compilation; density/PBR records
  guide authoring without silently overwriting fitted masses or materials.
- Existing optional hinges, cloth and soft bodies now reject implicit activation,
  zero-step runs, nonfinite parameters and incompatible moving components.
- [Container-task verification](../TASK_EVALUATION.md): fixed duration, release,
  containment, withdrawal, optional closure and sustained stability. References
  and keyframed/state-edited trajectories cannot be called solver task success.

## Next research steps and decision rule

1. Freeze a small public multi-scene benchmark and comparator revisions before
   candidate tuning. Separate single-photo inputs from video inputs. Confirm data
   access and redistribution terms; keep reconstruction and sealed evaluation
   observations distinct. Source-data gaps remain blocked cells, not exclusions.
2. Run a matched agent A/B with and without geometric feedback, same initial scene,
   observations, model settings and budget. Coarse cabinet silhouettes already fit;
   prioritize actual errors (textiles/accessories/reflections) and finer reviewed
   object/part boundaries rather than optimizing the coarse diagnostic alone.
3. Add a geometry-reference adapter (e.g. Pi3X) only as a separate video arm,
   retaining scale uncertainty and the known-dimension constraints.
4. Connect the task evaluator to a recorded MuJoCo robot episode; verify actual
   contacts and complete object vertices from the engine. Then test initial-pose
   and physical-prior sensitivity under a frozen task denominator.
5. Address single-assembly/no-furniture workflow coverage and complete-geometry
   landmark bindings. Keep flexible mechanisms optional and compare their added
   computational cost separately.

Promote an improvement only after source-fit and applicable physics/quality gates
pass with retained failures. A SOTA claim needs shared-baseline benchmark evidence;
the present one-room development checks do not supply it.

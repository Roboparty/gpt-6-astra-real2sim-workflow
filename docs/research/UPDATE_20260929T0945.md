## Single-part structural support

A one-part assembly legitimately has no internal joint. It now accepts explicit
empty declared/audited joint lists while retaining ownership, source-landmark,
floor-support, view and hash requirements. For multi-part assemblies, every
declared pair/anchor/tolerance must have one finite, passing measured check.
Missing or duplicate constraints and changed tolerances are rejected.

31 new contract cases passed, the 21-case single-assembly suite passed after its
synthetic measurement fields were made explicit, and the original structure
contract regression remained compatible. An actual Blender 4.5.3 one-part audit
and review passed: zero joints, zero cross-assembly pairs, three isolated views.
[Engine receipt](evidence/heartbeat_0945/single_part_blender.json).
Empty rooms and arbitrary unsupported hanging objects remain outside this scope.

tolerances and horizontal support planes fixed, and invalidates old fit results.
Unsupported active dynamics or collision-proxy recipes are rejected.

## Preventing export-induced modifier drift

The shared exporter previously cleaned/reoriented base meshes before evaluating
their modifiers. This can change Solidify/Bevel output geometry even when no
object transform changes. It now preserves any mesh datablock used by modifiers,
including data shared with an unmodified object, and checks evaluated bounds again
after other cleanup. Zero-area triangles are still excluded from OBJ output; this
does not promise universal topology repair or full interchange equivalence.

An [immutable two-condition test](EXPORT_MODIFIER_PROTOCOL_20260929.json) used the
retained authored `build_render/0003/scene.blend`, not the previously cleaned
delivery artifact. Old export changed the bed bounds by **8.7228 mm**, shoe-cabinet
bounds by 2.0000 mm and wardrobe bounds by 1.4999 mm. The new export's maximum bound
difference was **5.96e-8 m**, below the unchanged 1e-5 m preservation gate; all input
hashes were unchanged. These are internal authoring/export differences, not
real-world measurement accuracy.

[Paired result](evidence/heartbeat_0945/export_preservation.json). The initial old-
worker deployment lacked its sibling import module and failed before geometry
execution; [that attempt](evidence/heartbeat_0945/export_preservation_first.json)
is retained. The retry restored matching helper files without changing exporter
logic or the evaluation gate. No historical model was overwritten.

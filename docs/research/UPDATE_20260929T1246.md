## Why a separate cohort

The existing three AHa development videos and all their failed camera attempts
remain unchanged. Their sparse annotations do not supply independently measured
geometry. The new [frozen protocol](TUM_REFERENCE_PROTOCOL_20260929.json) selects
TUM `freiburg1_desk`, `freiburg3_structure_texture_far` and `freiburg3_cabinet`:
three different scene/object setups, not three proven independent rooms or homes.
TUM publishes RGB-D observations and mocap camera trajectories; its default data
license is CC BY 4.0. [Official dataset](https://cvg.cit.tum.de/data/datasets/rgbd-dataset),
[sequence catalogue](https://cvg.cit.tum.de/data/datasets/rgbd-dataset/download).

The intended input is six unchanged RGB frames per sequence at fixed temporal
fractions. Depth and mocap files belong in a separate evaluator directory.
This is logical separation, not an OS sandbox. No GT coordinates have been used
to fit a reconstructed model. Model pretraining exposure and room independence
remain unknown; this is development/reference preparation rather than a sealed
qualification benchmark.

## Trajectory evaluator ready, accuracy still unmeasured

`tools/evaluate_tum_trajectory.py` evaluates translation-only trajectories against
a frozen expected RGB timestamp list. It matches predictions and GT independently
to those timestamps within 20 ms, without interpolation or offset fitting, using
one-to-one monotone maximum coverage and then minimum total temporal offset.
All expected observations stay in the report. Missing prediction/GT frames leave
full-denominator ATE null rather than silently evaluating only successful poses.

It reports independent implementations of rigid SE(3) ATE and globally aligned
Sim(3) ATE. The latter is an added monocular diagnostic: its scale is fitted from
GT, not recovered metric scale. SE(3) error in metres is interpretable only when
the predictions already use metres. This is not a claim to run the official TUM
script; that script's scale is an explicit input parameter. Orientation/RPE and
dense depth accuracy are not implemented by this evaluator. [Official tools](https://cvg.cit.tum.de/data/datasets/rgbd-dataset/tools).

Twenty-five tests passed on the existing remote runtime, including known rigid
and similarity transforms, reflections, degenerate geometry, missing-frame
coverage, time association checked against small exhaustive cases, real CLI
provenance hashes and refusal to overwrite a report. These are mathematical and
contract tests with synthetic trajectories, not a successful reconstruction.
[Test log](evidence/heartbeat_1246/trajectory_tests.txt) ·
[source hashes and setup failures](evidence/heartbeat_1246/tool_checks.json).
